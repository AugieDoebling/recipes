# Pretzel Salad

# Ingredients

* 2 cups crushed pretzels
* 1/2 cup sugar
* 3/4 cup melted butter
* 1 (8oz) package cream cheese
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 (9oz) container frozen whipped topping
* 1 (6oz) box strawberry Jello
* 2 cups heated pineapple juice
* 2 (1 lb) packages frozen strawberries in juice

# Instructions

1. Mix crushed pretzels with 1/2 cup sugar and melted butter
1. Spread in a 9x13 pan and bake at 350°F for 10 minutes; cool
1. Mix cream cheese, 1 cup sugar, and whipped topping; spread over pretzels
1. Dissolve Jello in heated pineapple juice and add strawberries
1. Let partially set (about 20 minutes) and spread over cream cheese mixture
1. Chill overnight
