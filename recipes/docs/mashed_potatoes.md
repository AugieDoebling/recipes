# Mashed Potatoes

# Ingredients

* 5 lbs potatoes, cooked and mashed
* 1 (8oz) package cream cheese
* 1 cup plain greek yogurt
* Salt
* Pepper
* Granulated garlic

# Instructions

1. Chunk potatoes
1. Boil potatoes until fork soft
1. Combine all ingredients and bake at 350°F until golden brown on top
