# Serrano Sauce

# Ingredients

* 11 ripe (red) serrano peppers 
* 1 habanero pepper
* 1/3 of a yellow onion
* 8 cloves garlic
* Cumin
* Oregano
* 1/2c vinegar
* 1/4c oat milk
* 1/4c water
* 2tbsp butter
* Salt
* Cilantro
* 1/4c lime juice

# Instructions

1. Brown onion and garlic in a pan
1. Add spices, cook for a moment
1. Add vinegar, milk, water, butter, salt, and peppers
1. Simmer for a few minutes
1. Pour into blender
1. Add lime juice and cilantro
1. Blend until smooth
